Engineering detail — how we solve the problem

Technical identification before shipment

Availability is only helpful if the delivered part is the correct construction and revision. Link each SKU to a controlled drawing, dimensions, material options and picking identifiers. Show stock and delivery claims only when verified by the operational system.

Storage and product preservation

Seal faces must be protected against chips and impact, while elastomers require suitable storage conditions and age control. Keep identifiable packaging and traceable variants to prevent mix-ups. Appropriate storage rules depend on component materials and shelf-life specifications.

Verification and traceability

Define acceptance criteria that are meaningful for each component: material identity, critical dimensions, face geometry, elastomer specification and functional checks where applicable. Inspection records should identify the relevant production batch and drawing revision. Product-specific certificates require actual supporting evidence.

Technical information we need

Define the relevant specification, controlled drawing revision, selected materials, required checks and acceptance criteria. A manufacturing or quality capability is not a product certification.

Engineering enquiry

Send us the seal reference, equipment data, drawing or dimensions. SAP Parts reviews the fit, operating duty and material pairing before proposing a verified replacement or an engineered alternative.
