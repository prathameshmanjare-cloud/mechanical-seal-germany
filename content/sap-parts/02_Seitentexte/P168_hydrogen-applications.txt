Hydrogen Applications

Technical introduction

Hydrogen sealing requires application-specific assessment of leakage, gas behaviour, materials and safety requirements. Pump seal designs do not automatically transfer to gas service.

Technical Overview

Hydrogen sealing requires application-specific assessment of leakage, gas behaviour, materials and safety requirements. Pump seal designs do not automatically transfer to gas service.

Selection and engineering checks

The following points help define the correct sealing solution and its limits.

• Gas purity.

• Pressure cycles. Use the actual pressure at the seal chamber and note transients.

• Temperature. Check continuous and start-up temperatures at the sealing interface.

• Permeability.

• Ignition risk.

• Approved test protocol.

Common Risks

A good material can still fail when the design or operating conditions are wrong. Do not imply hydrogen qualification or regulatory approvals without actual testing.

Engineering approach

SAP Parts reviews the operating duty, checks the available dimensions and material options, and identifies the information needed before a qualified solution can be proposed. The aim is reliable operation with a practical total cost.

Engineering detail — how we solve the problem

Hydrogen duty and qualification

Hydrogen applications require a defined pressure and temperature envelope, leakage target, materials assessment and attention to permeation and safety. Existing liquid-service seal experience does not establish hydrogen suitability. Development must use application-specific validation.

Gas sealing and leakage control

Gas applications require an arrangement designed for the relevant leakage and lubrication regime. Do not transfer assumptions from a conventional liquid-lubricated seal. Specify gas composition, pressure variation, cleanliness, temperature, speed and safety requirements.

Engineering a material decision

A material change should begin with a failure mechanism and measurable acceptance criteria. Evaluate the face pair, corrosion behaviour, thermal properties, geometry, production route and total installed cost. A lower purchase price is useful only when fit, reliability and required qualification are maintained.

Technical information we need

For an application-specific recommendation, please send: existing drawing or seal reference; shaft diameter and mating seat dimensions; pressure and temperature at the seal; fluid composition, contaminants and solids; speed, operating cycle and shaft movement; present material configuration and observed failure pattern.
