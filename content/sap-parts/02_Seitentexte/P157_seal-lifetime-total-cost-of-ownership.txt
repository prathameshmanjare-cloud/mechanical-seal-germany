Seal Lifetime & Total Cost of Ownership

Technical introduction

The purchase price is only one part of a sealing system cost. Downtime, labour, spare parts, lost product and additional energy can be much more expensive.

Technical Principle

The purchase price is only one part of the cost of a mechanical seal. A failure can stop a production line, waste product, create cleaning work and require urgent maintenance. The value of reliable sealing is therefore often much higher than the price of the component.

Materials And Design

Total cost of ownership can include the seal price, installation time, support-fluid use, spare-part stock, planned maintenance, unplanned downtime and any product loss. The relevant costs vary by plant and application.

Engineering Conditions

A more expensive material may reduce total cost if it extends service life in a demanding duty. In another application, a well-selected lower-cost cast alloy or standard material pairing may meet the same requirement. The decision should use operating data and a documented comparison.

Reliability And Cost

SAP Parts focuses on material development, design selection and manufacturing efficiency. We assess opportunities to lower cost without presenting unverified service-life results as proven.

Selection and engineering checks

• Expected life.

• Service hours.

• Downtime cost.

• Replacement labour.

• Process loss.

• Inspection frequency.

Engineering detail — how we solve the problem

Material pairing and surface integrity

Specify the rotating and stationary faces as a pair. Hardness alone does not determine suitability: thermal conductivity, friction behaviour, corrosion resistance, abrasives, surface flatness and lubrication regime also matter. Evaluate the actual product grade and manufacturing route before substitution.

Engineering a material decision

A material change should begin with a failure mechanism and measurable acceptance criteria. Evaluate the face pair, corrosion behaviour, thermal properties, geometry, production route and total installed cost. A lower purchase price is useful only when fit, reliability and required qualification are maintained.

Verification and traceability

Define acceptance criteria that are meaningful for each component: material identity, critical dimensions, face geometry, elastomer specification and functional checks where applicable. Inspection records should identify the relevant production batch and drawing revision. Product-specific certificates require actual supporting evidence.

Technical information we need

For an application-specific recommendation, please send: existing drawing or seal reference; shaft diameter and mating seat dimensions; pressure and temperature at the seal; fluid composition, contaminants and solids; speed, operating cycle and shaft movement; present material configuration and observed failure pattern.

Engineering enquiry

Send us the seal reference, equipment data, drawing or dimensions. SAP Parts reviews the fit, operating duty and material pairing before proposing a verified replacement or an engineered alternative.
