Tribology Expertise

Technical introduction

Tribology helps us understand the contact between rotating and stationary sealing faces. We use this knowledge to examine friction, lubrication, wear and surface quality.

Technical Overview

Tribology helps us understand the contact between rotating and stationary sealing faces. We use this knowledge to examine friction, lubrication, wear and surface quality.

Selection and engineering checks

The following points help define the correct sealing solution and its limits.

• Face material pair. Specify the grade and pairing rather than a broad material family.

• Loading.

• Speed. Calculate relevant sliding speed and review starts and stops.

• Medium. State the full medium composition, including particles and cleaning fluids.

• Surface finish.

• Operating temperature. Check continuous and start-up temperatures at the sealing interface.

Common Risks

A good material can still fail when the design or operating conditions are wrong. No claim of unique discovery or patented principle is made.

Engineering approach

SAP Parts reviews the operating duty, checks the available dimensions and material options, and identifies the information needed before a qualified solution can be proposed. The aim is reliable operation with a practical total cost.

Application boundary

The exact solution depends on process data. A material or arrangement mentioned on this page is not a universal recommendation. We confirm dimensions, media compatibility and qualification requirements for the specific product.

Engineering detail — how we solve the problem

Engineering a material decision

A material change should begin with a failure mechanism and measurable acceptance criteria. Evaluate the face pair, corrosion behaviour, thermal properties, geometry, production route and total installed cost. A lower purchase price is useful only when fit, reliability and required qualification are maintained.

Verification and traceability

Define acceptance criteria that are meaningful for each component: material identity, critical dimensions, face geometry, elastomer specification and functional checks where applicable. Inspection records should identify the relevant production batch and drawing revision. Product-specific certificates require actual supporting evidence.

A useful engineering enquiry

Send the current seal or equipment reference, shaft and seat dimensions, operating pressure at the seal, temperature, speed, medium, solids, duty cycle and photographs or drawings. We review geometry, materials and duty before recommending a replacement, altered design or development project.

Technical information we need

Define the relevant specification, controlled drawing revision, selected materials, required checks and acceptance criteria. A manufacturing or quality capability is not a product certification.

Engineering enquiry

Send us the seal reference, equipment data, drawing or dimensions. SAP Parts reviews the fit, operating duty and material pairing before proposing a verified replacement or an engineered alternative.
