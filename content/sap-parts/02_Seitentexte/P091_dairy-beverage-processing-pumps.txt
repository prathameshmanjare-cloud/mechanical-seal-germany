Engineering detail — how we solve the problem

Hygienic design and cleanability

Sealing surfaces and secondary-seal grooves should be assessed for cleanability, chemical resistance and potential product hold-up. Confirm CIP/SIP conditions when relevant, including temperature, cleaning chemicals, time and pressure. Hygienic suitability and any food-contact compliance must be documented for the exact material and component, not inferred from the category name.

Material pairing and surface integrity

Specify the rotating and stationary faces as a pair. Hardness alone does not determine suitability: thermal conductivity, friction behaviour, corrosion resistance, abrasives, surface flatness and lubrication regime also matter. Evaluate the actual product grade and manufacturing route before substitution.

Secondary seal material selection

Check chemical compatibility against the complete fluid mixture, cleaning agents, exposure time and operating temperature. Consider swelling, compression set, extrusion gaps and dynamic movement where applicable. The polymer name by itself does not define the performance of the finished compound.

Technical information we need

For an application-specific recommendation, please send: existing drawing or seal reference; shaft diameter and mating seat dimensions; pressure and temperature at the seal; fluid composition, contaminants and solids; speed, operating cycle and shaft movement; present material configuration and observed failure pattern.
