Technical introduction

Every real, orderable SAP Parts replacement variant must have a permanent unique SKU. The SKU must identify its actual configuration.

Technical Overview

Selection and engineering checks

The following points help define the correct sealing solution and its limits.

• Construction.

• Exact dimensions.

• Material pairing. Specify the grade and pairing rather than a broad material family.

• Elastomer. The exact compound and cure system can change chemical compatibility.

• Spring material. Specify the grade and pairing rather than a broad material family.

• Verified replacements.

Common Risks

A good material can still fail when the design or operating conditions are wrong. Do not use one SKU for different material pairings or different installed heights.

Engineering approach

SAP Parts reviews the operating duty, checks the available dimensions and material options, and identifies the information needed before a qualified solution can be proposed. The aim is reliable operation with a practical total cost.

SAP Parts engineering support

We can help review drawings, dimensions, operating conditions and the material configuration before quotation.

Application boundary

The exact solution depends on process data. A material or arrangement mentioned on this page is not a universal recommendation. We confirm dimensions, media compatibility and qualification requirements for the specific product.

Engineering detail — how we solve the problem

Replacement verification workflow

An OEM number is an identification starting point, not automatic proof of interchangeability. Verify equipment variant, revision, shaft and housing geometry, seat, face materials, elastomers, operating conditions and available space. Only publish cross-references supported by verified product records.

Stationary interface and seating geometry

Confirm seat outside diameter, axial thickness, installation depth, anti-rotation method, elastomer seat and housing tolerance. The counterface must remain square to the shaft and must not be distorted by uneven mounting force. A visually similar ring can have a different functional geometry.

Engineering a material decision

A material change should begin with a failure mechanism and measurable acceptance criteria. Evaluate the face pair, corrosion behaviour, thermal properties, geometry, production route and total installed cost. A lower purchase price is useful only when fit, reliability and required qualification are maintained.

Technical information we need

For an application-specific recommendation, please send: existing drawing or seal reference; shaft diameter and mating seat dimensions; pressure and temperature at the seal; fluid composition, contaminants and solids; speed, operating cycle and shaft movement; present material configuration and observed failure pattern.

Engineering enquiry

Send us the seal reference, equipment data, drawing or dimensions. SAP Parts reviews the fit, operating duty and material pairing before proposing a verified replacement or an engineered alternative.
